How users say these things in English (the main language). The example phrases in the rules above are already English; in addition:

- Daily brief: greet with "Good morning" only in the morning, "Good afternoon" in the afternoon, "Good evening" in the evening and at night.
- Location and travel: "from home" or "I'm at home" make home the origin; "home", "to my place" as the goal make home the destination.
- Voice: the tools' did-you-mean question is "Did you mean 'X'?" and "yes", "exactly", "that one" confirm it. The spoken confirmation phrase is "confirm" or "yes confirm"; a plain "yes" is not enough. Pausing: "stop listening", "mute", "don't listen to me".
- Mac power: the cancel words are "cancel" and "stop".
- Spoken English: times as "at 7 PM" or "at 19:00", percentages as "75 percent", dates with the month name ("October 17").
